# Official J Law Checklists (超績交易員指南)

## Pre-buy checklist (all must check out)

- Completed the Train the Trader Buy System lessons
- Breakout of Pivot Point (PP) or Pocket Pivot (口袋樞紐點)
- Pivot / pocket pivot has run ≥ 3 days
- Volume dried up during pivot development (VDU/VC)
- Pivot range < 10% (tight, not loose)
- Relative Strength (RS) meets buy criteria; RS line near/at new high
- 50-day and 200-day MA both rising; price above both
- Price ≤ 8% above the 50-day MA
- Not a penny / low-quality stock (低價股/莊家股 rejected)
- Pattern identified; base built long enough; base tight, not loose
- Within 5% of the breakout point (ideally ≤ 2–3%) — never chase > 5%
- Technical stop-loss usable and ≤ 8% away
- Sector is strong; stock among the strongest leaders of that sector
- Not a low-volume stock; fundamentals support
- Stop-loss order set at/after order submission
- Market regime: easy-money vs blood-sweat; extra care in blood-sweat; if the
  easy-money run has been long, stay alert
- "If I could only buy 10 stocks this year, would this be one?"
- Risk-reward ≥ 2:1; clear position-management plan

## Pre-sell checklist

**Profit-locking sells (鎖定獲利式賣出):**
- Gain ≥ 1× the initial stop distance
- Gain ≥ your average win rate, or ≥ 2× it
- Weakening signals appeared
- Exhaustion gap-up at highs (消耗性裂口上漲) or climax run (急漲性走勢)
- Rule of thumb: +15–20% from breakout → sell at least 20–33%, sometimes 50%

**Risk-control sells (控制風險式賣出):**
- Stop-loss hit; or down > 8%
- Sell warning signal or "egg reaction" (雞蛋反應)
- Market index shows reversal warnings
- Using margin while stock/index acts against you
- Market entered blood-sweat period
- Right after buying, price breaks the 20-day or 50-day MA on heavy volume
- Largest drop in months on big volume
