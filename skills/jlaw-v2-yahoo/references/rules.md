# J Law's Rules (official PDFs)

## The 38 Mindset Rules (超績交易思維)

1. Every trade must have a stop-loss — know your stop before buying.
2. Max loss per trade must not exceed your average win rate.
3. Never average down — adding to a loser enlarges an error.
4. Scale up only when trading well; cut exposure fast when trading badly.
5. Go over-weight only when very in-sync; cut immediately if the trend turns.
6. Don't over-analyze into inaction.
7. Never carry a large position through an earnings release.
8. Never let a big profit turn into a loss.
9. Move your stop to breakeven ASAP.
10. If price action looks abnormal, sell first, ask later — missing 10
    opportunities won't hurt; one big mistake will.
11. After a breakout buy, watch for the next follow-through add point.
12. Buy in the order breakouts trigger — no personal favorites.
13. Review holdings daily; always think in risk-vs-reward terms.
14. Demand a perfect pattern — reject flawed setups.
15. Volume is fuel — rising volume on/after breakout is a good omen.
16. Never chase > 5% above the breakout point — wait for the next setup.
17. Don't aim to sell at the top: +15–20% from breakout → sell ≥ 20–33%,
    sometimes 50%.
18. Hold your strongest stocks longer — but sell fast when they act weird.
19. Pick strong stocks in strong sectors — all tailwinds on your side.
20. Stay highly alert after a long uninterrupted rally — raise cash, cut
    leverage, sell into strength.
21. A triggered buy point ≠ must buy — weigh breakout quality and market
    backdrop (early bear market = high failure odds).
22. Be careful with margin — no leverage until emotions, technique, charts and
    market judgment are mastered.
23. In a strong sector, trade only the strongest names — never laggards.
24. Long new-high stocks, short new-low stocks.
25. The market is never wrong; your opinion often is — never argue with the tape.
26. No method wins all the time; accept losses; <50% win rate still wins with
    risk management.
27. If stock and market both act right, don't rush to sell everything — partial
    sells lock gains; let the rest run.
28. Human emotion is the biggest enemy — use a plan and system to remove
    subjectivity.
29. Big moves take time — institutions accumulate over weeks/months; give young
    uptrends time.
30. Track few stocks, not many — keep the watchlist manageable.
31. Trade actively in easy-money periods; hibernate in blood-sweat periods.
32. Reject low-quality selection criteria: tips, hope, financial media,
    penny/operator-controlled stocks.
33. Know your blind spots and error patterns — post-trade analysis every time.
34. Don't overtrade — the goal is making money, not trading.
35. Keep RRR ≥ 2:1.
36. Don't chase quick riches — over-focus on reward precedes blowups.
37. Position size neither too large nor too scattered.
38. Keep discipline — consistent principles, no constant style-switching.

## The 18 Winner Rules (18個買賣股票贏家守則) — condensed subset

1. Every trade must have a stop.
2. Max loss per trade ≤ average win rate.
3. Never average down.
4. No paralysis by analysis.
5. Never let a big profit become a loss.
6. Missing 10 opportunities < 1 major mistake.
7. Review holdings daily, always in risk-reward terms.
8. Never chase > 5% above the breakout point.
9. Don't seek to sell at the exact top.
10. Buy strong stocks in strong sectors.
11. Reject tips, hope, media-driven and penny-stock buying.
12. Careful with margin — it can K.O. you.
13. Long new highs, short new lows.
14. The market is never wrong; your view often is.
15. Accept losses — you won't always be right.
16. Focus on long-term consistency — <50% win rate still wins with risk control.
17. Keep RRR > 2:1.
18. Keep trading discipline — consistent principles, no style-switching.
