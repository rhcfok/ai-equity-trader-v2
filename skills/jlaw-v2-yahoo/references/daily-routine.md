# Daily Trading Routine (每天交易部署流程, official PDF)

## Pre-market 開市前

- Check positions; adjust stop orders as needed
- (HK) Check overnight futures + IG HSI futures quote; check overnight US close
  and current US futures
- If no major adverse overseas move: place **Buy Stop** orders per the pre-written
  plan/watchlist, with stop-loss orders attached
- Review existing Buy Stops — cancel or re-price if the stock's action deteriorated
- Mental rehearsal: visualize today's best/worst case and your instant response

## During market hours 交易時段

- No impulsive buying — act only on pre-planned alerts with excellent price
  behavior; otherwise let stop orders execute the plan
- Check holdings for anomalies (profit-take / add / stop)
- Scan the 52-week-high list — add strong, high-volume names to the watchlist
- Observe the tracked leader list to absorb market feel (盤感)
- Don't stare at the tape; avoid financial news — everything is already in the plan

## After close 收市後

- Run the screener templates (per Buy System 1.1 / 1.3a / 1.3b) — hunt new setups
- Sort candidates into watchlists; write down near-breakout names and pre-set
  Buy Stops + stops
- Review the indices (US + home market): improving or deteriorating? adjust strategy
- Review holdings: adjust stops / add-points / profit targets
- Write the trading plan, update the trading log, do the **MRA**
  (Measure → Review → Adjust)
