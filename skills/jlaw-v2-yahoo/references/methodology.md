# J Law Methodology (distilled from Train the Trader 2.0 + Master Your Trade)

J Law trades momentum / trend-following growth stocks (Minervini–O'Neil lineage),
US and HK markets. Four pillars + one regime filter.

## Regime filter (gates everything)

- **躺賺時期 (easy-money trending market)**: trade aggressively; gains come several ×
  faster and easier. But after a long uninterrupted rally, stay highly alert —
  sell into strength, raise cash, cut leverage.
- **血汗錢時期 (blood-sweat choppy market)**: hibernate. Stand aside and wait for a
  clear trend.
- MYT weekly 股市分析 labels the market **Risk-On** (focus: narratives, favored
  industries, buy strong sectors/stocks) / **Neutral** (rotation, raise caution) /
  **Risk-Off** (protect downside, raise cash).

## Pillar 1 — Buy System (買入系統)

1. **Judge the trend**: stocks move through four stages (basing → advancing →
   topping → declining). Only buy Stage-2 uptrends.
2. **Judge the market index**: never fight the tape. Know the 4 market-top signals
   and 8 bear-market-bottom signals.
3. **Stock selection**: Relative Strength (RS) line at/near new high; fundamentals
   accelerating (earnings/sales, company in growth phase); avoid the P/E trap.
4. **Entry patterns**: VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern), The Power Play,
   VCP Breakout Style pivot, other valid breakout bases.
5. **Order placement**: Buy Stop at the pivot + stop-loss submitted simultaneously.
6. **Multiple Edge entry**: stack the nine edges (trend lines, channels, MAs, gap
   behavior…; see glossary).

## Pillar 2 — Sell System (賣出系統)

- Risk management and stop-loss principles (initial stop ≤ 8%).
- Sell into weakness (defensive) and into strength (offensive profit-taking).
- Re-entry after selling; the sell warning signals (賣出警號), incl. the
  "egg reaction" (雞蛋反應 — stock cracks right after breakout).

## Pillar 3 — Holding System (持貨系統)

- Position sizing: sleep-well size; neither too large nor over-diversified.
- Add only when the trade works; move stop to breakeven ASAP.
- Shakeout (OS/SO) handling and re-entry; low-volume pullback (LVP) after breakout
  is healthy.
- Never hold a large position through earnings.

## Pillar 4 — MRA (Measure, Review, Adjust)

Daily journal → measure stats (win rate, avg win, avg loss) → post-mortem every
trade → adjust. Keystone metrics: max loss per trade ≤ average win; RRR ≥ 2:1.
With those, <50% win rate still compounds.

## Short selling (bonus)

Long stocks making new highs, short stocks making new lows — new highs mean the
market expects a better future; new lows, a worse one.
